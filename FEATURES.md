# ImHungry Product Feature Scope

This file is the source of truth for ImHungry's agreed product features. Read it before planning architecture, persistence, tools, or implementation work in this repository.

The current scope contains four feature groups. Earlier discussions referred to five groups, but the former fourth and fifth groups were merged into Adaptive Diet Coaching.

## 1. Diet Setup

- User profile and dietary preferences
- Starting and goal weight
- Weight-loss or maintenance goal
- BMR and TDEE calculation
- Initial calorie and macro targets
- Activity level as nutrition context

## 2. Food Tracking and Calculation

- Log, edit, remove, and replace foods
- Daily calorie and macro totals
- Food nutrition lookup and estimation
- Recipe nutrition and per-serving calculation
- Saved recipes and frequently eaten foods
- Hydration tracking

## 3. Meal Planning and Food Decisions

- Meal and recipe recommendations
- New food suggestions
- Restaurant menu recommendations
- Food substitutions
- Eating-out and social-event planning
- Recommendations based on current intake and goals

Restaurant discovery remains a stretch goal.

## 4. Adaptive Diet Coaching

- Hunger, cravings, and food fixation
- Nighttime snacking and recurring problem situations
- Portioning and substitution strategies
- Handling missed targets and high-intake days
- Weight and fat-loss progress
- Scale fluctuation interpretation
- Self-reported energy, recovery, hunger, and body-image feelings
- Muscle preservation
- Adjusting calorie and macro targets
- Sustainable consistency and avoiding compensatory restriction
